## subject_definitions｜主体定义

### 段落 1
**原文：** <Subject 1> (S1) is the main contestant, visually derived from <Picture 1>. Preserve her identity and overall appearance from <Picture 1>, including face, hairstyle, hair color, skin tone, apparent age, body proportions, physique, clothing, and any accessories visibly present in the reference. Do not introduce new wearable accessories that are not present in the reference.

**关键词与术语：**
- contestant /kənˈtestənt/ n.：参赛者
- visually derived from /ˈvɪʒuəli dɪˈraɪvd frəm/ phr.：视觉上以……为来源
- identity /aɪˈdentəti/ n.：人物身份
- skin tone /skɪn toʊn/ n. phr.：肤色
- apparent age /əˈpærənt eɪdʒ/ n. phr.：外观看起来的年龄
- body proportions /ˈbɑːdi prəˈpɔːrʃənz/ n. phr.：身体比例
- physique /fɪˈziːk/ n.：体型
- wearable accessory /ˈwerəbl əkˈsesəri/ n. phr.：可佩戴配饰

**整段翻译：**  
`<Subject 1>`（S1）是主参赛者，其视觉身份来自 `<Picture 1>`。必须保留 Picture 1 中的人物身份与整体外观，包括脸、发型、发色、肤色、外观年龄、身体比例、体型、服装，以及参考图中清楚可见的所有配饰。不要加入参考图中不存在的新可佩戴配饰。

## summary｜摘要

**原文：** [reference generation] Create a 20-second, 16:9 horizontal, highly realistic live-action Chinese television water-obstacle variety show.
